Try the connected flow

  1. Customer: tap Book Your Service and send a request.
  2. Owner: it appears under Needs Action. Open it and schedule a slot.
  3. Customer: the booking now shows the confirmed appointment.
  4. Owner: Start Job, then Complete Job. The report lands in the customer's Documents and the next service date updates.
  5. Customer: accept or decline quote Q-2031; the owner sees the decision instantly.
